Payments

Mut Mee Garden Guest House prefers to use an online credit card payment system. This is provided by PaysBuy which is a Bank of Thailand approved payment gateway. PaysBuy is also approved by Visa and Mastercard. It is safer than using a regular credit card machine as it allows for direct authorisation from your home bank. We prefer PaysBuy over a conventional card reader as many credit card machines in Thailand do not accept international pin numbers. Mut Mee Garden Guest House will never see your card numbers or details.
