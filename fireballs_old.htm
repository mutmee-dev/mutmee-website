26th October: Arriving in Style...

If you are coming
to Nong Khai to
see the Maekong
Fireballs on the 26th of October,
why not travel down to Phon Phisai (where the Fireballs can be seen) in style. Pao is organising an elegant afternoon dinner cruise on our boat "The Nagarina".

There are places for just sixty people to sail the 55 kilometres from Nong Khai to Phon Phisai along the Maekong River. During the approximately four hour trip you will be served a beautiful dinner, given light refreshments along the way and offered a glass of wine or champagne. Leaving Nong Khai at midday you will arrive in Phon Phisai in the late afternoon.

Pao is organising a magnificent feast on board featuring a range of international Asian dishes together with some local Isan recipes. And she loves making sweets, so there will be a range of delicious desserts too. Moreover, we are planning to set up a bar down on the lower decks for you to purchase our classic Maekong cocktails. There can't be a more fun way to arrive at Phon Phisai to see the festival!

We arrive in Phon Phisai in the late afternoon, in time to see the hustle and bustle of the festival along the riverbank. The Fireballs usually first appear around 7.30 pm after dark and shoot up once in a while until around 10.00pm. At that time we will start the journey home.

And to get home you have a choice. Either you can lounge on cushions on the deck of the Nagarina for the 7-8 hours it takes to fight the current upstream through the moonlit night back to Nong Khai. Or for those who would rather not, we are putting on a bus which leaves Phon Phisai at 10.00pm to bring you straight home to Mut Mee.

So come join the festival in style, and put your name down for a touch of elegant fun! Dress to match the occasion if you wish! Just send us an email to mutmee@mutmee.com and we will send you instructions and place your name on our list!

The price of the trip including, boat trip, meal, wine or champagne (but excluding cocktails), and pickup by bus to return to Mut Mee, or lounge trip back on the boat to Nong Khai is 2500 Baht per person. Payment to be made in advance. Booking closes 24th of October 2007. For further information write to the email address above and Julian will reply quickly.
