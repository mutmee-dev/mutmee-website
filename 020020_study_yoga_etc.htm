Yoga

For more than eight years,Yoga Masters, Pancho and Beatrix have been offering a seven day yoga intensive course, designed to provide an understanding of the basic principles of yoga. The instruction covers Asana (yoga postures), Dharana and Dhyana (meditation), and Pranayama (breathing exercises).

The programme is designed to give students a complete understanding of the total yogic system, so that they may continue with their own independent practice at the end of the week.

Mut Mee

Have you ever thought about learning yoga, or would you like to be physically fitter? Surrounding Mut Mee Guest house are a number of people offering courses or consultations that may greatly enhance your sense of well-being...
Instruction is given for around two and a half hours in the early morning and three to four hours in the afternoon. If you wish, you can join in an optional meditation period from 2.00 pm to 3.00 pm in the afternoon.

Courses take place throughout most of the year and start on the 1st, 10th and 20th of the month. For further information please contact Pancho and Beatrix through their website at www.pantrix.net.

Fitness

Mark is a qualified fitness trainer from the United States. He offers tailor made sessions that employ a wide range of techniques to increase strength and flexibility.

Working with any individual irrespective of their age or starting levels of fitness, he aims to steadily improve their physical status in a series of relaxed yet structured one hour classes.

Mark takes great care that the training is adequately constructive yet not overly taxing. If you would like to contact Mark, send an E-mail to Mut Mee.

Reiki

Beatrix Schilcher is a Master in the Usui System Of Reiki Healing. This channeling of vital energy through the hands has become widely popular around the world, over the last few years.

Acting as a conduit for readily available universal energy which pervades the atmosphere around us, the practitioner of Reiki treatment will help to alleviate the roots of ill-health and stimulate a feeling of general well-being.
If you are interested in learning how to practice the art of Reiki, then Beatrix offers initiations to the levels of Reiki I and Reiki II. Reiki has long been the province of specialised esoteric healers but has lately emerged as a simple, easy to learn technique readily accessible to the average person. For further information please contact Beatrix at www.pantrix.net.

Astrology

Beatrix, who has engaged in a vast study of Evolutionary Astrology for more than 20 years, has extensive experience in astrological counselling. She offers this in three forms: Birth Chart Readings, Transit and Progression Readings, and for couples Synastry and Composite Readings.

If you would like to book an appointment, or simply wish to know more about Astrology contact her at www.pantrix.net.
