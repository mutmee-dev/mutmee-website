14th October: Through the Night
to see the Fireballs...

If you are coming
to Nong Khai to
see the Maekong Fireballs, give yourself an extra chance of seeing them by travelling at night back from Phon Phisai to Nong Khai on our boat "The Nagarina", past spots where fireballs have often been seen in the past. .

In the afternoon we are organising trucks to take you from Nong Khai down to Phon Phisai, where you can enjoy all the fun of the festival there. At 9.00pm you board the Nagarina which will make its way slowly along the Maekong river, past places where fireballs have been seen in the past. Though there is no guarantee, this gives you the best chance of glimpsing them.

Pao is organising a wonderful dinner on board, and refreshments along the way. Moreover, we are planning to set up a cocktail bar down on the lower deck for you to purchase our classic Maekong cocktails. There can't be a more fun way to travel through the night along the Maekong on this most mystical of nights! We will pass villages agog with spectators and quiet spots full of hush and calm...

The journey upstream will necessarily be quite slow, so we expect to arrive home in Nong Khai at around 3.00am. However, we will arrange cushions for you to lounge on to watch as the hours go by and we will have regular refreshments for you along the way.

On arrival in Nong Khai, the Nagarina will dock at her usual birth, just below Mut Mee Guest House, so you will not have more than a minute's walk to get home to bed. It will be a long night, but surely worth it for that magical glimpse. Certainly, you will have a better chance of seeing them than just standing among the crowds in Phon Phisai!

So come join the festival in style, and put your name down for a touch of mystical fun! Just send us an email to mutmee@mutmee.com and we will place your name on our list!

The price of the trip including, truck down to Phon Phisai in the afternoon, boat trip leaving Phon Phisai at 9.00pm, meal, (but excluding cocktails), is 1200 Baht per person. Payment to be made in advance. Booking closes 12th of October 2008. For further information write to the email address above and Julian will reply quickly.
